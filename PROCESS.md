# Process overview

## What I built

I built a course planner app, that can understand the ANU program, and how to plan the courses, and manage the requirements and prerequisites rules applied on the app. Also majors and specialisation are available to be seen.

## How I got here

It started when i almost didnt finish the program on time (2 years), because i miss-manage to plan the courses majors / specialisation. From that moment on, i want to capture my mistake back there and make a tool that can help to visualize on how to plan the courses based on my experience. I also want to gamify it so it will be fun and enganging to use.

- I start by giving the idea on how the design should be and the flow of the app.
- Giving the idea then discuss with the opus 5.5, then it gave me the brief and we wrote that down into claude.md as harness making it possible to re-iterate without always having in the same session over and over. ( [docs/progress](docs/progress/2026-09-25-skill-tree-planner.md) )
